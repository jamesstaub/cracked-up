cracked-up graph inspector

Live demo Docs Audio is off. The graph is drawn; the meters wait for Start.

The graph on the right is drawn by cracked-up/inspect from the scope this page builds. Each box has a meter on its output, and a connection moves only while signal is going through it. Click a box for its parameters, a scope and a spectrum.

"Add a voice" re-runs the page's patch with one more chain, through the same build() the live demo uses. Nodes that were already there stay where they are. The drive starts bypassed; its button switches it in.

What the patch contains:

  • three drum samplers mixed on a bus called drums;
  • a saw bass through a filter that an lfo sweeps, a drive, and a tapedelay, which is a macro: open it to see the feedback loop inside;
  • a monosynth lead that splits into a dry and a delayed branch;
  • a sine that nothing ever triggers, so its chain stays still;
  • five sequences that trigger the drums, the bass and the lead.

Drag to pan. Pinch, or hold Ctrl or Cmd and scroll, to zoom. Double-click a macro or a scope to show only its inside; the trail at the top leads back out.
